I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at [Università della Svizzera italiana (USI Lugano)](https://www.usi.ch/) under the supervision of [Fabrizio Mazzonna](https://sites.google.com/site/fabriziomazzonna/home). 

> Research fields: health economics • labour economics • ageing

I completed the [Swiss Program for Beginning Doctoral Students in Economics](https://www.szgerzensee.ch/) and hold an MSc in Political Economy from [LSE](https://www.lse.ac.uk/european-institute) and an MSc in Economics from the [University of Verona](https://www.dse.univr.it/?ent=home&page=dipartimento&lang=en). Before my PhD, I worked as a Research Analyst at [The European House - Ambrosetti](https://www.ambrosetti.eu/en/our-story/).

You can find my CV [here](/cv/).
